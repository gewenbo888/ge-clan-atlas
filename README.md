# Ge Clan Atlas · 戈氏图谱

A structured atlas of the Chinese surname 戈 (Ge): origin, dynastic timeline, migration routes, regional distribution, notable figures, cultural symbolism, and the careful disambiguation from the homophonous 葛.

## Modules

1. **Origin & Etymology** — meaning of 戈 (dagger-axe), three pathways into the surname pool (Xia state of Ge, Zhou halberd-keeper officials, Qing-era Manchu sinicization), with evidence-grade tagging
2. **Historical Timeline** — 8 dynastic anchor moments from Xia (~2070 BCE) → present with per-event impact list
3. **Migration Map** — interactive SVG of 4 migration waves (Han, Tang/Song, Qing, 19c+ diaspora) across 10 province nodes
4. **Clan Genealogy** — 9-node interactive tree showing the 3 main branches (Henan original · Jiangsu/Zhejiang Tang-era · Northeast Qing-era sinicized) and their sub-branches
5. **Notable Figures** — filterable era tabs; cards explicitly tagged 戈 vs 葛 to prevent the common conflation (includes Zhuge Liang, Ge Hong, Ge You as 葛-disambiguation cards)
6. **Cultural & Symbolic Meaning** — the 戈 radical, war-related compounds (干戈, 倒戈, 戈壁, 化干戈为玉帛), and what the surname carries aesthetically
7. **Regional Distribution** — 9-province bar chart with summary, branch breakdown, and headline stats
8. **Comparison Module** — three-column side-by-side of 戈 (gē) vs 葛 (gě) vs 各 (gè), with origin / population / concentration / figures

## Features

- **Bilingual EN ↔ 中文** instant toggle, localStorage-persisted
- **Evidence-grade tagging** (strong evidence · plausible reconstruction · documented · traditional account)
- **戈 vs 葛 disambiguation** built into the figures and comparison modules — explicit, not hand-wavy
- **Classical ink-wash aesthetic** — gold + vermilion + jade on deep ink; Noto Serif SC + EB Garamond + JetBrains Mono
- **Personal note**: built by Gewenbo (戈文博), bearer of the Jiangsu branch lineage. Gewenbo appears as one of the contemporary figures.
